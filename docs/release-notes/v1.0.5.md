## Telescope mount control over Bluetooth

Move an iOptron mount from the dashboard while watching the live preview, so you can frame a target, keep it there, and capture it from your phone. The camera connects through the ESP32 **SmartEQ-RJ9** adapter ([esp32-ioptron_smart_eq_controller](https://github.com/aviralverma-8877/esp32-ioptron_smart_eq_controller)).

### Connecting

- **Bluetooth page** (new icon in the nav bar): scans for nearby devices; tap the adapter to connect. The first connection pairs with it (no PIN, about 30 seconds). After that it's listed as **Last used** and reconnects in a couple of seconds.
- **Stays connected.** If the link drops (adapter power blip, out of range), the camera reconnects by itself for up to 2 minutes and shows "reconnecting…" meanwhile.
- The adapter accepts one connection at a time, so disconnect on the Bluetooth page before using SkySafari or iOptron Commander with it.

### Controls on the live preview

While a mount is connected, its controls appear on the preview itself, so you never scroll away from the image:

| Where | Control |
|---|---|
| Edges | Hold **N / S / E / W** to move; release to stop |
| Top-left | Slew speed, 1x to Max |
| Top-right | **Track** — the mount turns with the sky to keep a framed star in place (green "Tracking" while on; tap again to stop) |
| Bottom-left | **Zero** — slew to the zero position (asks first) |
| Bottom-right | **Stop** — halts moves and slews (tracking keeps running) |

The mount's state, RA and Dec show just below the preview.

- **Nudging keeps tracking on**, so you can re-center a tracked star.
- **Safe if your phone drops off.** The mount only moves while a button is held. If the phone stops checking in (WiFi drops, page closed), the camera stops the move by itself within about a second.

Tested on an iOptron SmartEQ Pro.

## Screen stays on

The dashboard now keeps your phone's screen from locking while it's open, so a long sequence or a tracked target stays in view. It starts after your first tap on the page. On some phones this pauses music that's playing while the dashboard is open.

## Also

- The nav bar stays pinned to the top of the screen while scrolling.
- Bluetooth is switched off on the stock image; the camera turns it on when needed. The package's permission rule now allows exactly `rfkill unblock bluetooth` for that, and the package depends on `bluez` and `rfkill` (both already on Raspberry Pi OS).
